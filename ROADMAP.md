# Wildmere Roadmap

A quiet phone-first valley survival sketch in Three.js.

## Done
- Mobile touch: left walk stick, right look, on-screen E/F/G/R/M/Tab.
- Core loop: gather, plant, fish, rest, build simple pieces, rain, day cycle.
- Many quiet landmarks with first-visit notes and notebook.
- Notebook with allowlisted notes and sanitized localStorage.
